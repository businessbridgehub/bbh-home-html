Privacy Policy

Learn how Business Bridge Hub collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

Business Bridge Hub ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy helps you understand how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you visit businessbridgehub.com or use our services. Please take a moment to read it carefully. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, we ask that you do not use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, and company name when you voluntarily submit it through our contact form, consultation requests, or service inquiries.

Usage Data

When you browse our website, we automatically gather basic details like your IP address, browser, operating system, and the pages you look at. We also track the dates, times, and websites that brought you to us. This information simply helps us see how people use our site so we can make it better and easier to navigate.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors come from. You can manage your cookie choices directly inside your browser settings. Just keep in mind that turning cookies off might change how some parts of our website work.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect from our users for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, comments, or questions
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To send administrative information such as updates, security alerts, and service-related communications
  • To analyze usage trends and improve site functionality
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
  • To protect the security and integrity of our website and services

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal details with outside companies to advertise to you.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

We implement industry-standard security measures — including encryption, secure servers, and access controls — to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, because no digital storage system or online transmission is completely bulletproof, we cannot guarantee your data is 100% secure.

Your data is stored on secure servers located in data centers that maintain high physical and digital security standards. We only keep your personal details for as long as we need them to do the work mentioned in this policy, or to follow local laws.

We might share your data only in the following specific situations:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, processing communications, or delivering services — provided they agree to keep your data confidential.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: If our business is ever sold, merges with another company, or passes on its assets, your data may be included as part of that transition.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website might include links to outside sites, plugins, or services. We don't control and aren't responsible for how these external websites handle your privacy, so we suggest reading their policies before sharing any data. Our site may use third-party services such as Google Forms for contact submissions and analytics providers for usage tracking.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

Based on where you live, you might have specific choices and rights over how your personal data is handled:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Restriction: You may request that we restrict the processing of your data.
  • Data Portability: You may request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: You may object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.

To make a request regarding your data rights, reach out to us at contact@businessbridgehub.com. Our team will respond as quickly as required by law.

7. Cookies Policy

We use cookies to help our website work better and to see how visitors interact with our pages. These are just small text files that your browser saves onto your device. Our site uses temporary cookies that vanish when you close your browser, as well as permanent ones that stay until you remove them. You can adjust how these work anytime through your browser settings. Essential cookies are required for basic site functionality; analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our site.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not meant for anyone under 16 years old. We never intentionally gather data from children. If we find out that a minor has shared their personal details with us, we will move quickly to delete that information. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in servers located in countries other than your own. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure that your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy regardless of where it is processed.

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever necessary. Any changes will be posted right here with a fresh effective date. If we make major updates to how we handle your data, we will let you know via email or put a clear notice on our website. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
