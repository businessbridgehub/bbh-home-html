The BBH Ecosystem

BBH products are WordPress themes and plugins developed by the Business Bridge Hub engineering team. Each product addresses a real-world need we encountered while building and optimizing WordPress sites for clients. From lightweight theme architecture to security toolkits, technical SEO and schema solutions, performance monitoring, Gutenberg blocks, content displays, and WooCommerce filtering — these tools are production-tested and built to integrate seamlessly with modern WordPress environments.

All BBH products follow WordPress coding standards, support the latest PHP versions, and are regularly updated to stay compatible with WordPress core releases.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are BBH products?

BBH products are WordPress themes and plugins developed by Business Bridge Hub. They include the BBH Lite theme, BBH SEO Toolkit, AJ Card Element, AJ Category Posts, AJ FAQ Block, AJ Square Testimonial Slider, and AJx Filter for WooCommerce. Each tool is designed to solve specific WordPress challenges around SEO, content display, e-commerce, and site management.

Are BBH plugins compatible with modern WordPress versions?

Yes. All BBH plugins are tested against the latest WordPress stable release and maintained for backward compatibility with WordPress 5.6 and above. We follow WordPress coding standards and release updates within 72 hours of major WordPress core updates.

Are BBH products free?

BBH Lite and all current BBH plugins are free and available on WordPress.org. Future premium products like BBH Pro Theme will offer advanced features under a paid license. Free products receive ongoing updates and community support.

Do BBH products work together?

Yes. All BBH products are designed to integrate seamlessly with each other and with most WordPress themes and plugins. There are no known conflicts, and we test each release against a matrix of popular plugins including WooCommerce, Yoast SEO, and Elementor.

How often are BBH products updated?

Security and compatibility updates are released as needed, typically within 72 hours of a WordPress core release. Feature updates follow a quarterly cycle. All updates are announced on our blog and via the WordPress plugin update system.

Can I contribute to BBH products?

Absolutely. All BBH products are open source and hosted on GitHub. We welcome contributions through pull requests, bug reports, and feature suggestions. Each repository includes contributing guidelines and a code of conduct.
