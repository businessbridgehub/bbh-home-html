Reduce avoidable maintenance surprises

Outdated components, untested updates, missing backups, and unclear ownership can turn small website problems into larger interruptions. A maintenance routine helps teams keep track of essential tasks and respond more deliberately when something changes.

Business Bridge Hub can discuss an ongoing support arrangement tailored to the website's complexity and operational needs. The exact frequency, response expectations, monitoring, and included work should be agreed in writing before service begins.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Have a question that is not covered here? Send it with your project details.

Are emergency fixes included?

Only if the plan explicitly includes them. Emergency response, recovery work, and larger development tasks should have clearly defined terms.

Do you guarantee my website will never be hacked or go offline?

No. Maintenance can reduce some risks and improve readiness, but no service can guarantee uninterrupted availability or complete protection.

How often are updates performed?

The schedule should reflect the site's needs and the agreed plan. Updates may require compatibility checks or staging tests before production.

Are hosting and premium plugin licenses included?

Not automatically. Hosting, paid licenses, and third-party fees should be listed clearly in the proposal if they are part of the arrangement.

Can you maintain a website built by someone else?

Usually, after reviewing its setup and access. Some legacy code or undocumented customizations may require an initial assessment before ongoing support can be scoped.
