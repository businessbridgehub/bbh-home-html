Make WordPress work the way your business needs

Off-the-shelf themes and plugins are useful starting points, but they do not solve every business requirement. When your site needs a tailored workflow, a more suitable layout, or a feature that existing tools cannot provide cleanly, custom development can close the gap.

Business Bridge Hub helps businesses and digital agencies plan and implement WordPress changes with attention to compatibility, security, accessibility, and maintainability. The right solution may be a focused code change, an integration, a custom plugin, or a broader development task.

Every website is different. Scope, access, timing, and expected outcomes are confirmed before implementation begins.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Have a question that is not covered here? Send it with your project details.

Can you work on an existing WordPress website?

Yes. Share the website and explain what needs to change. The first step is to assess the current setup and determine a sensible scope.

Can you build a custom plugin?

Custom plugin work can be considered when the requirement is clear and suitable for a focused, maintainable solution.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. You can discuss a defined development task, overflow work, or a longer-term technical support arrangement.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the requirements, complexity, existing code, and testing needed. Send a project summary to request a scope and estimate.

Will you work directly on my live website?

That depends on the task and available setup. Where practical, changes should be developed and tested on staging or a local environment before deployment.
